SENT NEWSLETTERS

Click any text on a box to edit it and click the photo to upload one. Hover a box for options: + add a member under it, ◀ ▶ move it left or right, ✕ remove it (and everyone under it).
Click + Add event to create one. On each event: 📷 Photos uploads pictures (you can pick several at once), 🎬 Video adds a video file (MP4 works best) or a video link, ✎ Edit changes the details or removes photos / videos, 🗑 deletes the event. Click a photo or video to view / play it. Video files are kept only in this browser.
Automatic columns come from the Projects tab (member is the POC or one of the Developers) and the Attendance tab (selected month). Your KPIs: click + Add KPI to create one with a target, then turn Edit KPI values ON and type each member's actual result for the month. Overall score = average of Attendance %, Avg project progress and each KPI's achievement (capped at 100%), using only the parts that have data.
Click a box to mark attendance: it changes WFH → WOS → VL → SL → UL → blank. Members come from the IGNITE Members tab (add or rename people there). Attendance % = (WFH + WOS) ÷ (WFH + WOS + SL + UL): only SL and UL lower it - VL and blank are not counted. Hover a box and click ✎ to add a reason - boxes with a remark show an orange ✎ (point at it to read it).
